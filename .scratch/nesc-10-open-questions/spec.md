## Summary
The philosophical heart: each open question presented in a fixed epistemic format that always ends by naming what remains unknown.

## Why
§7: format each as *Question · What science knows · What the evidence suggests · Where the evidence disagrees · What remains unknown.*

## Scope / acceptance criteria
- [ ] Open Questions surface (hub entry [004]) lists questions across concepts; each concept's Case File also surfaces its own.
- [ ] Each question renders all five sections in order; the **What remains unknown** section is always present and visually distinct (evidence-red UNKNOWN treatment).
- [ ] Claims in "what science knows / evidence suggests / disagrees" carry citations where they assert fact (NESC-04).
- [ ] Data comes from the structured `open_questions` schema (NESC-03).

## Notes
